**Diablo 4 Assist 0.1.5** fixes the check boxes in the settings window.

**Download** `D4Assist.zip`, unzip it anywhere and run `D4Assist.exe`. Updating from an earlier version: close the program and copy only the new `D4Assist.exe` over the old one. The zip's `d4assist.json` would reset your settings.

### Changes
- Pressing Space on a check box in the settings now tells you whether it's checked or not checked. Before, your screen reader said nothing and you only found out by moving away and back (thanks for the report).
- Everything from 0.1.4 is still here: Ctrl+Alt+Shift+G turns the guide on and off, also while a game menu is open.

Para actualizar sin perder tus ajustes, cierra el programa y copia solo el nuevo `D4Assist.exe` encima del anterior.
