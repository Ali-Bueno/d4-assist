**Diablo 4 Assist 0.1.4** lets you turn the guide on and off from the keyboard.

**Download** `D4Assist.zip`, unzip it anywhere and run `D4Assist.exe`. Updating from an earlier version: close the program and copy only the new `D4Assist.exe` over the old one. The zip's `d4assist.json` would reset your settings.

### Changes
- **Ctrl+Alt+Shift+G** does the same as left on the D-pad: it turns the guide on and off, and in the desert sandstorm quest it mutes and unmutes the ping that leads you between shelters. Unlike the D-pad, it also works while a game menu is open.
- Everything from 0.1.3 is still here: the program looks for the game on all your drives, asks for the folder if it can't find it, and the settings have a Browse button for it.

**En español:** la 0.1.4 permite activar y desactivar la guía desde el teclado con **Control+Alt+Mayúsculas+G**, igual que la cruceta izquierda (también silencia la ruta de la tormenta de arena). A diferencia de la cruceta, funciona también con un menú del juego abierto. Para actualizar sin perder tus ajustes, cierra el programa y copia solo el nuevo `D4Assist.exe` encima del anterior.
