**Diablo 4 Assist 0.1.14** fixes a prisoner that followed you around in dungeons.

**Updating:** with 0.1.7 or newer, the program offers this version itself when it starts (or from the tray icon's menu, "Check for updates"). From an older version, close the program and copy only the new `D4Assist.exe` over the old one; the zip's `d4assist.json` would reset your settings.

### Fixed
- A freed prisoner who follows you (in the Hallowed Stones nightmare dungeon, even through the portal to the next level) kept playing the prisoner sound at your side, and the exploration guide kept saying there was an objective right behind you. Now a prisoner who walks along with you goes quiet and the guide drops it. Prisoners still waiting to be freed sound as before.

This change hasn't been tested in the game yet. If you see it again, please run the program with `--debug` and send me `d4assist-log.txt` and `d4assist-log.old.txt` from the program's folder.
